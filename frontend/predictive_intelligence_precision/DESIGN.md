## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the calculated precision, forward-looking intelligence, and absolute reliability demanded by enterprise retention leaders and revenue operations executives. Inspired by the dense, ultra-crisp interfaces of modern developer and analytics powerhouses, the visual style combines structured enterprise ergonomics with luminous telemetry cues. 

The aesthetic is anchored in an elevated technical minimalism enriched with low-contrast glassmorphism and luminous signal accents. Every pixel serves cognitive speed: high-density data matrices, clean division of structural panels, luminous telemetry state indicators, and subtle tactile feedback mechanisms that give operators certainty when navigating complex churn cohorts. The emotional tone evokes unshakeable confidence, predictive clarity, and strategic mastery over complex customer lifecycles.

## Colors

The palette leverages a dark-first operational posture optimized for sustained analytical scrutiny, supported by a mirrored, ultra-clean light mode. 

### Core Palette
- **Primary Electric Blue (`#1687FF`):** Powers focal actions, primary selections, predictive paths, and active timeline scrubbers.
- **Secondary Cyan (`#18C8E8`):** Emphasizes live streaming signals, dynamic telemetry nodes, and cohort intersections.
- **Tertiary Violet (`#8B5CF6`):** Dedicated to machine learning layers, automated retention interventions, and algorithmic probability calculations.
- **Neutral Deep Navy (`#071A2B`):** Establishes the structural canvas, base layer, and dominant dark viewport.

### Specialized Surfaces & Risk Signaling
- **Surface Foundations:** Dark mode nests depth via `#071A2B` (base canvas), `#0F2338` (structural containers), and `#162E48` (elevated panels). Light mode inverts this architecture cleanly with `#F8FAFC` (base canvas), `#F1F5F9` (card surfaces), and `#FFFFFF` (elevated focal cards).
- **Retention & Risk Tiers:**
  - *Critical Risk (`#EF4444`):* Severe churn probability (>75%), SLA breaches, and contract drop-offs.
  - *Elevated Risk (`#F59E0B`):* Moderate volatility (40–74%) and declining product usage.
  - *Healthy Retained (`#22C55E`):* Stable accounts (<40% churn risk), expansion trajectories, and positive health benchmarks.
  - *Expansion Mint (`#6EF3C5`):* High-velocity upsell indicators and net retention growth vectors.
- **Structural Borders:** Subtle, non-intrusive boundaries driven by `#E2E8F0` in light environments and `#1E3A5F` / `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` in dark environments.

## Typography

The typographical structure implements a rigorous dual-tier strategy designed for high-density enterprise environments. 

- **Primary Headers (Manrope):** Geometric yet friendly contours provide authoritative visual anchoring on high-level executive summaries and module titles without feeling rigid.
- **System Interface & Body (Inter):** High x-height and neutral grotesque terminals guarantee effortless scanability across multi-layered data tables and nested churn factor breakdowns.
- **Telemetry & Quant Metrics (JetBrains Mono):** All raw metrics, ARR impacts, churn percentages, cohort sizes, and algorithmic confidence scores must be rendered with tabular numbers (`tabular-nums`) to prevent horizontal jitter during live telemetry updates and enforce alignment in numerical columns.
- **Micro-labels (`label-caps`):** Used strictly in uppercase for section categorizations, column headers, and status groups to maintain clear typographical scale separation.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout follows a strict 12-column responsive fluid grid structured around a consistent 4px sub-grid. It prioritizes information density while maintaining strict hierarchical breathing room.

### Breakpoints & Canvas Logic
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12-column dynamic grid, `gutter-desktop` (1.5rem), `margin-desktop` (2rem). Supports multi-pane work surfaces: fixed 240px navigation dock, collapsible 320px telemetry inspector, and fluid central analytical canvas.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column layout, `gutter` (1rem), `margin-tablet` (1.5rem). The telemetry inspector collapses into an overlay drawer; data grids prioritize primary risk factors.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, `gutter` (1rem), `margin` (1rem). Complex data visualizers reflow into stacked priority KPI cards with horizontal carousel tabs for multi-dimensional cohort graphs.

### Spacing Intent
- `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) are reserved for internal component architecture: icon-to-label gaps, badge padding, and segmented control frames.
- `space-md` (12px) and `space-lg` (20px) govern inter-card structures and analytical module padding.
- `space-xl` (32px) defines boundary clearance between divergent functional domains on the dashboard.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is articulated through luminous layering and surface tone differentiation rather than heavy drop shadows, reinforcing a modern glass-analytical feel.

### Surface Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Viewport Canvas):** Base background (`#071A2B` in dark mode, `#F8FAFC` in light mode). Completely non-interactive background.
- **Tier 1 (Surface Containers):** Main analytics panels and dashboard widgets (`#0F2338` dark / `#FFFFFF` light). Framed by a 1px boundary: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)` in dark mode and `#E2E8F0` in light mode.
- **Tier 2 (Elevated Active Modules):** Hovered cards, flyout menus, and active cohort inspectors (`#162E48` dark / `#FFFFFF` light). In dark mode, applies a soft blue-tinted ambient glow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(7, 26, 43, 0.6), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(22, 135, 255, 0.15)`.
- **Tier 3 (Floating Overlays & Modals):** Command palettes, retention intervention builders, and toast notices. Utilizes frosted glass: `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` over a semi-translucent shell (`rgba(15, 35, 56, 0.82)`), edged with an assertive hairline border: `1px solid rgba(24, 200, 232, 0.25)`.

### Ambient Illumination
Interactive chart nodes and alert triggers project faint directional radial glows matching their severity (e.g., a critical node projects a 12px ambient blur of `#EF4444` at 20% opacity), producing an active instrumentation sensory feel.

## Shapes

The geometric architecture is balanced, refined, and distinctly engineered. It applies a baseline `roundedness: 2` to yield smooth, modern contours without softening the technical authority of an enterprise telemetry tool.

- **Micro Components (0.375rem / 6px):** Form controls, inputs, data grid cells, and segmented toggles use tight rounding to preserve layout compactness.
- **Pills & Status Indicators (`rounded-full`):** Churn confidence meters, retention status badges, and user tags take pure pill silhouettes to starkly differentiate metadata tags from rectangular cards.
- **Analytical Containers & Metric Cards (`rounded-xl` / 1.5rem):** Primary modules and chart visualizer frames utilize generous curvature, softening complex density into organized information islands.
- **Modals & Drawers (`rounded-2xl` / 2rem):** Floating workflows and command panels use wide radii to emphasize separation from the underlying analytics canvas.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary:** High-luminance Electric Blue fill (`#1687FF`) with white text, 0.5rem radius, and an ambient inner highlight `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`. On hover, transitions to `#1472D6` with a subtle electric glow.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background enclosed by a 1px border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` (dark) or `#CBD5E1` (light). Text is anchored to the primary foreground.
- **AI Action Triggers:** Tertiary violet background (`#8B5CF6`) with a luminous gradient sheen (`linear-gradient(135deg, #8B5CF6 0%, #18C8E8 100%)`), used exclusively for automated retention triggers and AI synthesis actions.

### Badges & Status Pills
- **Risk Indicators:** Structured as a composite element featuring a 6px glowing status dot alongside monospaced percentage metrics. Critical risk employs an ultra-low opacity red shell (`rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.12)`) bounded by a subtle `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.3)` stroke, paired with `#EF4444` text. Moderate and healthy states follow identical logic using amber and green tokens respectively.
- **Metadata Tags:** Neutral pill badges with muted background tones (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`) and micro-typography (`label-caps`).

### Data Tables & Interactive Lists
- **Layout:** Row heights locked to 44px (compact) or 56px (expanded). Hairline divider borders (`1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`).
- **Behavior:** Subtle full-row background transition to `rgba(22, 135, 255, 0.04)` on hover, with interactive right-aligned action buttons revealed smoothly on cursor focus.
- **Numerical Alignment:** Monospaced figures right-aligned with fixed character widths to ensure cross-row scanning parity.

### Form Inputs & Filters
- **Fields:** Dark slate interior (`#0B1E32`), surrounded by a 1px boundary (`#1E3A5F`). Text set to `body-md` in crisp white. On focus: instant border transition to `#1687FF` paired with an external focus halo (`0 0 0 3px rgba(22, 135, 255, 0.2)`).
- **Segmented Range Sliders & Selectors:** Enclosed pill track with a sliding `#162E48` active thumb, housing concise monospaced timeline marks (e.g., `30D`, `90D`, `180D`, `1Y`).

### Metric & Analytics Cards
- **Structure:** Encased in `rounded-xl` frames with 1.25rem internal padding. Header features the metric label in `label-caps` muted grey alongside an optional trend chip.
- **Telemetry Readout:** Primary metric presented in `data-metric` tabular typography, directly coupled with a sparkline or directional drift chevron.
- **Predictive Trajectory Visualizer:** Mini-chart modules embedded within cards utilize semi-transparent area gradients running from `#1687FF` (at 0.25 opacity) down to `#1687FF00` at the baseline, overlaid with 1.5px high-clarity SVG spline paths.
